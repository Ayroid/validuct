# Launch Strategy: Deploy Now + Build in Public

## The Strategic Decision

Three options considered:
1. Create a landing page depicting current + future aspects
2. Launch current webapp with better descriptions
3. Wait and build further

**Recommendation: Deploy Now + Build in Public**

---

## Why Not Just a Landing Page

A landing page for an idea validation platform without a working product is deeply ironic. You'd be asking people to validate your idea... on a landing page... for a platform that validates ideas. The meta-irony would not be lost on your target audience.

## Why Not Wait to Build More

You could spend 2-3 months building AI analysis, structured voting, waitlists... and then discover nobody cares. You'd be violating the core principle your product teaches: **validate before you build**.

## Why Deploy Current + Iterate

You have a **functional product**. It works. Ship it, get real users, and let their behavior tell you which differentiation features actually matter.

---

## The Hybrid Approach

### Week 1: Deploy & Soft Launch

```
├── Deploy backend (Railway/Render)
├── Deploy frontend (Vercel)
├── Add email/password auth (expand user base)
├── Seed 10-15 quality ideas yourself
└── Soft launch on r/SideProject, Indie Hackers
```

### Week 2-3: Learn & Build ONE Differentiator

```
├── Watch what users actually do
├── Talk to 5-10 users directly
├── Build the SINGLE highest-impact feature:
│   → Recommended: AI Idea Analysis (wow factor + immediate value)
└── Iterate based on feedback
```

### Week 4+: Product Hunt with a Story

```
├── "We built an idea validation platform..."
├── "...and validated it with 100 real users"
├── "Here's what we learned, here's V2"
└── Launch with traction proof + differentiation
```

## The Meta-Validation Play

Use Validuct to validate Validuct's roadmap.

Post your own differentiation features as ideas on your platform:
- "AI-powered competitor analysis for every idea"
- "Collect waitlist signups directly on ideas"
- "Expert badges for qualified validators"

See which ones get the most engagement. Let your users vote on your roadmap. It's authentic, it's on-brand, and it gives you real signal.
